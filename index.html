FAQs

Where is Ali Estate located?

Our office is in Arif Town, Kamahan, Lahore. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Lahore's main societies, including DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg, Johar Town and Arif Town.

Do you help with paperwork?

Yes. We check ownership, NOC, taxes and society dues, and handle the agreement, transfer and registry for you.

Can I rent or invest through you?

Yes. We handle renting and property management as well as buying and selling, and we give investment guidance on plots, files and rental yield.

How do I book a visit?

Message us on WhatsApp at +92 323 4083930 or use the form below, and an advisor will arrange a site visit.
